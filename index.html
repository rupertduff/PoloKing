On the field

Four ways to play

Quick Match

Set up a one-off match between two club teams. Pick the sides, then play the chukkas.

International

Set up a one-off match between two international teams.

World Cup

Play as a men’s or women’s team. Line up the squad, choose the level, and take them toward the final.

Player Career

Create your player and your team. Build a string of horses, raise your handicap toward 10 goals, and compete for the Triple Crown.

In the match

Learn the rules while you ride

Tutorials walk through the right of way. If an opposition player enters that line, it is a foul and the line turns red.

  • Classic. Drag to aim. A longer drag means more power. Release to strike.
  • Buttons. Hit, shoot, and sprint. Hold longer for more power.
  • Camera. Follow your rider, or pull back to a broadcast view of the field.
